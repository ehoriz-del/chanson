What is Chanson?

French chanson is more than music. It is storytelling through melody, poetry, and emotion. Each song carries a memory of love, life, and a moment in history.

Legendary Voices

Édith Piaf

The voice of France. Known worldwide for "La Vie en Rose".

Charles Aznavour

The poet of chanson. His songs tell stories of love and life.

Featured Songs

La Vie en Rose

Édith Piaf · 1947

A song about seeing the world through love. "Life in pink" represents a beautiful view of life.

La Bohème

Charles Aznavour · 1965

A nostalgic song about youth, dreams, and memories of Paris.

Stories Behind Songs

Why do French songs remain timeless? Because chanson tells personal stories. A simple melody can preserve a lifetime of emotions.

French Expression

Voir la vie en rose

Literally: "To see life in pink"

Meaning: To see life optimistically.
