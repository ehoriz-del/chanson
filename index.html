ABOUT CHANSON.NET

100 songs, presented one day at a time.

Chanson.net is an independent editorial guide to French-language song. Every day, one recording offers a doorway into a voice, a period, and a piece of cultural memory. The collection repeats after 100 days, so a missed song always returns.
